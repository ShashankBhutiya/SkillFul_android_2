# SEO Launch Checklist

## Domain and canonical setup
- Set `VITE_SITE_URL` to the final production domain.
- Force one canonical host version only.
- Confirm all canonicals resolve to production URLs.

## Search engine setup
- Verify the domain in Google Search Console.
- Submit `/sitemap.xml` in Google Search Console.
- Verify the domain in Bing Webmaster Tools.
- Submit `/sitemap.xml` in Bing Webmaster Tools.
- Request indexing for the homepage, categories hub, and top category pages.

## Technical checks
- Confirm `/robots.txt` returns `200`.
- Confirm `/sitemap.xml` returns `200`.
- Confirm `/llms.txt` returns `200`.
- Confirm all category pages return `200`.
- Confirm 404 pages are `noindex`.
- Confirm structured data is present on homepage, category pages, and blog articles.

## Priority pages to submit first
- `/`
- `/categories`
- `/categories/parenting-skills-quiz`
- `/categories/effective-communication-quiz`
- `/categories/interview-prep-quiz`
- `/categories/salary-negotiation-quiz`
- `/categories/leadership-workplace-quiz`
- `/categories/stock-market-quiz`
- `/blog`

## Growth and authority
- Publish founder posts linking to the category hub and 2 to 3 flagship category pages.
- Add the site to relevant startup, education, and productivity directories.
- Reach out to parenting, career, and communication creators for backlinks or mentions.
- Track impressions and CTR in Search Console weekly.
